## Frame and datum

- Body: Mars 2000 sphere, R = 3,396,190 m.
- Longitude: planetocentric, east-positive, -180 to 180. Latitude: planetocentric.
- Heights: metres above the Mars areoid (MOLA geoid), as delivered in the CTX DEM.
- Coverage: 77.1605 to 77.6990 degrees E, 18.2110 to 18.7212 degrees N.
- Projection of the source mosaics: equirectangular, latitude of true scale 0, central longitude 0 (the `Eqc_latTs0_lon0` in the labels).
- Every raster layer is cropped to the DEM's outer edges, so a texture coordinate maps to lon/lat with one linear formula.
- Scene metres: x = R cos(lat_c) (lon - lon_c) pi/180, z = -R (lat - lat_c) pi/180, y = (h - h_ref) times exaggeration.

## Processing

TODO(verify) each step against `scripts/prepare-data.mjs`.

1. Download the DEM and ortho GeoTIFFs (about 9.7 MB and 36 MB) with geotiff.js. Raw files stay in `data/raw/`.
2. DEM: crop, store as little-endian Int16 (`dem.i16`) with scale, offset and nodata -32768 recorded in `dem_meta.json`.
3. Ortho and THEMIS: resample to the DEM extent with sharp and write JPEGs. THEMIS brightness is relative and not calibrated to temperature.
4. Geology: read the USGS features, reproject to lon/lat (east-positive), clip to bounds, write `geology.json` with unit descriptions.
5. Waypoints and traverse: clip to bounds, write `traverse.json`. Names go to `nomenclature.json`.
6. Rover images: queried per sol, cached as `rover_cache/<sol>.json` with camera, date and credit.
7. Write `data/source_manifest.json` and `data/validation.json`.

## Validation file

`data/validation.json` compares the DEM elevation sampled at each Perseverance waypoint with the elevation recorded for that waypoint. Both are heights above the areoid. A small bias is expected because the rover elevation comes from a different product. Result (14 Nov 2026, 708 waypoints inside the DEM): mean residual −0.89 m, median −1.06 m, standard deviation 3.25 m, 95th percentile |residual| 6.44 m, maximum |residual| 11.72 m. The landing waypoint (sol 13) differs by +2.46 m. The five largest residuals cluster near 77.258°E, 18.462°N (sols 1451–1463), a steep area, so they reflect local relief rather than a systematic georeferencing offset.

## Known data limits

- 20 m posting cannot show boulders or metre-scale scarps. Slopes are averaged over about 40 to 60 m.
- DEM and ortho are CTX products. Co-registration with the THEMIS mosaic (100 m) is coarse.
- The geologic map is an interpretation at 1:75,000. Unit boundaries are not precise to 20 m.
- Rover photos are historic, not live. Their location is tied to the sol through waypoints.
- Outside the DEM there is no data and the app says so.
