4Calculations

4.1 Walking speed per segment (Tobler 1993, scaled for a suited crew)
v(θ) = f × 6 km/h × e^(−3.5 × |tan θ + 0.05|) mean outbound grade tan θ = 0.012 → v = 0.6 × 6 × e^(−3.5 × 0.062) = 2.89 km/h
4.2 EVA duration
t = Σ (segment length ÷ v) + dwell = 37 min + 34 min + 30 min = 101 min = 1 h 41 min
4.3 Suit battery
used = P × t ÷ C = 250 W × 1.68 h ÷ 2000 Wh = 21.1 % left = 78.9 % (reserve 20 %, pass)
4.4 Mechanical work of climbing (lower bound on effort)
W = m × g_Mars × ascent = 180 kg × 3.721 m/s² × 71 m = 47.6 kJ
4.5 Comms line of sight with planetary curvature
drop = d² ÷ 2R = (1790 m)² ÷ (2 × 3,396,190 m) = 0.47 m ray sampled every 10 m on the 20 m DEM; vertices 23 to 51 hidden; stretch 1.1 km > 0.5 km limit (fail) with a 10 m mast: hidden stretch 0.0 km (pass)
