## What to show (only what is built)
- The whole real Mars from NASA data, orbit to ground (Cesium, Mars Trek).
- Point at a spot and ask: MarsAgent answers at the cursor and acts on the map.
- Every answer computed: narrated steps, formulas with real numbers, an exportable report.
- Real simulations on real terrain: lander through the Mars 2020 EDL phases, two crew walking at 600x.
- Verdicts GO / HOLD / NO GO with the limiting reason (comms line of sight in the story).
- Earth is about 11 light-minutes away (JPL Horizons), so the crew plans on Mars.
- Evidence: terrain agrees with 708 Perseverance waypoints, median 1.1 m.
