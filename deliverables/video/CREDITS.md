# Credits (demo video)

Footage: real screen capture of the live HeyMars app (https://web-production-9b58e394.up.railway.app, 15 Nov 2026),
recorded with Playwright and the Chrome DevTools screencast at 2880x1620. Idle waits are cut out (jump cuts) and some
clips play up to 1.7x faster; nothing is staged or composited into the app's picture. The film adds captions, callouts,
the EDL rail and the camera framing on top.

Data shown in the app and credited on screen: NASA MRO CTX (terrain and imagery), Mars Odyssey (THEMIS), USGS
(SIM 3464 geologic map), Mars 2020 Perseverance (waypoints, raw images), NASA JPL Horizons (Earth light time),
JPL Mars Trek (planet imagery and terrain). EDL phase times: NASA/JPL Mars 2020 Perseverance landing press kit
(`public/data/global/edl_mars2020.json`). No NASA logos or insignia appear.

Music: original bed generated by code in this folder (`tools/music.py`, numpy synthesis), written with AI assistance
(Claude Code). No samples, nothing to license. Mixed low; not ear-checked by the builder.
No voice, no AI voice.

Illustrations (title, light-time diagram, MarsAgent diagram, EDL rail, callouts, end card): drawn in HTML/CSS/SVG for this
film. Icons: Lucide (ISC licence), taken from the repo's lucide-react package (`assets/icons.json`).

Fonts: Schibsted Grotesk, Martian Mono, Michroma (SIL Open Font License 1.1), the app's own fonts.

AI-generated images: none in this film. If one is added, it must carry a visible "AI-generated" mark in frame and be
listed here with the tool and prompt.

Tooling: HyperFrames (render), GSAP (motion), ffmpeg, Playwright, Puppeteer.
